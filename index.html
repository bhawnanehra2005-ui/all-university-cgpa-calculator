How to Convert CGPA to Percentage?

Most Indian universities (like CBT, AKTU, and other central/state universities) use a standard formula to convert CGPA into an equivalent percentage for placements and higher education forms.

Percentage (%) = CGPA * 9.5

Example Calculation:

  • If your CGPA is 8.0
  • Calculation: 8.0 * 9.5 = 76%
